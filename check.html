Reproduce proves the number is real: a reviewer installs the open engine, re-runs it on this bundle's own recorded inputs, and the answer has to land inside the band the bundle sealed in advance.     The bundle, the reviewer guide, and the open engine are public: how to reproduce it yourself.

    An honest note on the signature. The key pinned in this checker is still SMRFORGE's public demo key — its private half ships in the open-source repo. So a passing signature here proves integrity (the bundle was not edited), not origin (that it came from us). Anyone can produce a demo-key signature. That changes the day we run the real signing ceremony offline and re-sign the published bundles; until then, we would rather say so than let a green check mean more than it should.
