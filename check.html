    How to trust this check. The key pinned in this checker is SMRFORGE's production signing key (fingerprint ed25519:cfb88804…d69b52c), generated in an offline ceremony. So a passing signature here proves both integrity (the bundle was not edited) and origin (that it was sealed by SMRFORGE). Don't take our word for the key: cross-check that fingerprint against our published trust root — the file at /.well-known/smrforge-trust-root and the DNS TXT record at smrforge-trust-root.smrforge.io. Origin holds only if they match.
