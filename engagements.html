How an engagement works

From your number to a reviewer’s re-run.

1Bring a number. Your k_eff, shutdown margin, or a result from any code.
2We re-derive it. On the open engine, escalated to OpenMC where it has to hold up.
3You get a sealed bundle. Signed, with the data pinned, the toolchain recorded, and the lineage you can walk end to end.
4Your reviewer re-runs it. Offline, with a checker that imports none of our physics. Try the checker.
What the seal proves, and what it doesn’t. A sealed bundle proves the number is unaltered and can be re-derived from its own recorded inputs — custody, not correctness. SMRFORGE results are screening-to-reference grade and advisory, honestly labeled on every result; a confirmation makes a number checkable, it is not a certification and nobody should license a reactor on it alone.
