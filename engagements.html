How an engagement works

From your number to a reviewer’s re-run.

Four moves. You make the first and your reviewer makes the last. The two in the middle both end in a seal.

01you

Bring a number.

Your k_eff, shutdown margin, or a result from any code. Tell us what it is, which code produced it, and who has to accept it.

02SMRFORGE

We re-derive it.

On the open engine, escalated to OpenMC where it has to hold up. Your inputs never get altered. Each one gets recorded and hashed.

03SMRFORGE

You get a sealed bundle.

manifest
  inputs/        sha256
  result.json    sha256
  provenance/    engine · library · toolchain · seed
signature      ed25519

Signed, with the data pinned, the toolchain recorded, and the lineage you can walk end to end.

04your reviewer

They re-run it.

$ smrforge verify
  OK · integrity + origin
$ smrforge reproduce
  re-derived within 3σ

Offline, with a checker that imports none of our physics. Verify works in a browser. Try the checker. Reproduce needs the open engine installed.

What the seal proves

Custody, not correctness.

A sealed number from a badly specified model is a faithfully preserved wrong answer. Every result says what grade it is. Here is the line, drawn plainly.

A confirmation proves

  • The number is unaltered since it was sealed, and anyone can check that offline.
  • It re-derives from its own recorded inputs on the open engine, within the committed band.
  • The toolchain that minted it is recorded inside the seal and covered by the signature.
  • The engine is benchmarked against public references on open ENDF/B-VIII data. Of record today, an IAEA-2D full-core closure of −3 pcm against the published reference (code verification, not measured validation) and a continuous-energy Monte Carlo floor of +108 pcm, signed.

A confirmation does not prove

  • That your model is right. We re-derive the number from your inputs. We do not certify the inputs.
  • Licensability. A confirmation is not a certification, and nobody should license a reactor on it alone.
  • Reference fidelity by default. SMRFORGE results are screening-to-reference grade and advisory, labeled for fidelity on every result. It is not a licensing code.
  • A solve in your browser. Verify runs offline anywhere. Reproduce needs the open engine.
Advisory, by design. A confirmation makes a number checkable. Your physicists still own the physics, your reviewer still makes the call, and the seal hands them something to check instead of someone to trust. Want to see the seal hold or fail before you send anything? Try to break it.
